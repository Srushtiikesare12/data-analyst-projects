College Performance \& Fees Analytics

2\. Fee Collection Analysis



\- MBA has the highest fee collection of ₹1,80,000.

\- BBA has the lowest fee collection of ₹1,00,000.



Recommendation: Monitor course-wise fee collection and improve fee follow-up processes.



3\. Pending Fees Analysis



\- MCA has the highest pending fees of ₹1,50,000.

\- BBA has the lowest pending fees of ₹20,000.



Recommendation: The college should prioritize fee follow-ups for courses with higher outstanding amounts.



4\. Pending and Partial Payments



\- The analysis identifies students whose fees are pending or partially paid.



Recommendation: The college should maintain regular payment reminders and follow-up records.



5\. Student Age Analysis



\- Latur has the highest average student age of approximately 22.67 years.

\- Satara has the lowest average student age of approximately 20.33 years.



Recommendation: Use student demographic information to understand the admission profile across cities.

Recommendation: Monitor withdrawn enrollments and understand the reasons behind student withdrawals.

Recommendation: Review academic performance across all courses and identify areas requiring improvement.

Recommendation: Provide additional academic support in subjects with lower average scores.



9\. Failed Subject Analysis
